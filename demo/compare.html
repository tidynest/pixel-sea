20 s seamless loop · 12 fps · click a style to switch

The one to ship. 64 colours cut from the render itself by median cut. Dither is visible as texture without speckling. Watch the cumulus in the water near the horizon, and the glitter road narrowing to a point under the sun.

Hard retro. Same sea, 16 colours. Heavy banding in the sky and much coarser dither, the deliberately old-hardware look. Cheapest file of the four.

Highest fidelity. 640×360 at 96 colours. Wave detail survives much further toward the horizon because each pixel row covers less sea. Nearly 4× the bytes of retro.

Reference, not a shipping option. Full 24-bit, no palette, no dither. Useful for judging whether the palette is losing anything. Mostly it is not, at 4× the size.
