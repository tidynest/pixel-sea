pixel-sea · themes

640×360 · 96 colours · 20 s seamless loop · same renderer, eleven data tables

dusk. Low sun, violet sky, warm cumulus over deep blue water. The clouds are visible in the water near the horizon; that reflection is the same function the sky uses, pointed along the mirrored ray.

dawn. Cold light just after sunrise with the last stars still out. At time_scale 0.26 the sea is quiet but not flat, so the low sun has ripples to catch. arctic is the calmest of the set now, at 0.18.

storm. Grey-green gale. The eye drops to 9 m so you sit down in it, the wave amplitudes roughly triple, and foam_k 2.2 keeps whitecaps breaking constantly. Flat extinction coefficients grey the light instead of reddening it.

tropical. Bright daylight, turquoise shallows, white cumulus. The sun sits about 10° up, low enough to lay a glitter path on the water while Fresnel still keeps the water's own colour carrying the frame.

night. Moon low over black water, silver road, full star field. The moon is the same light source at intensity 3.2 with a cool tint and a fifth of the angular radius.

ember. Sun on the horizon through heavy dust. The red comes from airmass_k [0.10, 0.42, 0.95], which scatters blue and green out of the beam. It is not a picked orange.

arctic. Cold low sun on flat calm, seen from 20 m up. Ice-blue water under an open sky, and the widest-feeling of the set because the high eye pushes the horizon far out.

mist. Cold sea haar with the sun buried in it. The grey is not painted on: airmass_k [0.30, 0.33, 0.38] is nearly flat, so extinction() greys the light instead of reddening it the way ember does. haze_k 0.55 is the highest in the set and piles the rest onto the water line.

noon. Sun high and hard over open ocean. At elev 0.330 it is the only theme with the light above ten degrees, which puts extinction() at airmass 2.2 against tropical's 3.5, so the disc comes out white without a colour being picked for it. The glitter road broadens toward the viewer instead of pinching to a point at the horizon, which is what every low-sun theme in the set does.

gloaming. The sun is a few minutes down. elev -0.055 puts it below the horizon row, so the disc is never drawn and the aureole is the only part of the light left, which is why the warm smear sits on the water line instead of above it. It is the floor of the same relation noon tests the ceiling of. At 9.45 MiB it is also the lightest loop in the set by four megabytes, because calm water and a star field a tenth of night's leave little per-pixel noise for deflate to choke on.

trough. The lowest eye in the set at 4 m, against arctic's 20. That is a distance knob and nothing more, since render() takes z from the row on flat water, so no crest can occlude anything behind it. What it does is bring the horizon in to 1440 m from 7200, which magnifies whatever the spectrum holds. The wavelengths here are about a third of dusk's to match: written with an ordinary 70 m swell it rendered as a sheet of glass, because from 4 m up a 12 m wave spans most of the frame.
